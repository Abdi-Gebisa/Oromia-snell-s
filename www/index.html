ABOUT THE PROJECT

Learning should be open to everyone.

OromiaSTEM is a free educational project designed to help secondary-school and university students understand science and mathematics through interactive visual experiments.

01

Learn actively

Interact with models instead of only reading descriptions.

02

Experiment safely

Explore idealized scientific models and compare outcomes.

03

Stay curious

Use the simulations as a starting point for questions and practical learning.

Educational note: simulations use simplified models. Real experiments may differ because of measurement uncertainty, friction, air resistance and other factors.
