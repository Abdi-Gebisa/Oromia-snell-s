QUICK REFERENCE

Laboratory Notes & Formula Reference

Use these relationships with the assumptions stated in each experiment. Check units before substituting values.

Kinematics

v = u + at

s = ut + ½at²

v² = u² + 2as

Dynamics & Energy

ΣF = ma

p = mv

W = Fd cos θ

K = ½mv²

P = W/t

Projectile Motion

x = v₀ cos θ · t

y = v₀ sin θ · t − ½gt²

R = v₀² sin(2θ)/g (equal launch/landing height)

Optics & Waves

n₁ sin θ₁ = n₂ sin θ₂

1/f = 1/u + 1/v

v = fλ

Electricity

V = IR

P = VI = I²R

Q = CV

Magnetism

F = BIL sin θ

ε = −N ΔΦ/Δt

Formula conventions can differ between textbooks. Follow the sign convention and definitions shown in the relevant lesson.

ABOUT THE PROJECT

Learning should be open to everyone.

OSTEM is a free educational project focused on helping Ethiopian secondary-school students understand Physics through interactive visual experiments, accurate calculations and guided investigation.

Educational note: simulations use simplified models. Real experiments may differ because of measurement uncertainty, friction, air resistance and other factors.
